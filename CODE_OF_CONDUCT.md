# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project. Be respectful,
assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and keep technical discussion
focused on improving the software. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's security
reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the
community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
